The Problem & Solution
📧
Problem
30% of freelancer time is wasted on admin chaos

Chasing clients for files, waiting on feedback for drafts, sending invoice reminders, and watching clients dig through email threads for "final_v2_final.pdf". Email is too chaotic; Jira/Asana is too heavy.

🧘
Solution
A shared client portal with versioned files, feedback threads & invoices

ClientFlow gives every project a clean shared workspace — one URL the client bookmarks. Files auto-version, feedback is threaded in context, invoices live alongside the work, and nothing lives in email.

How It Works
STEP 01
🔗
Create a project workspace

Freelancer creates a project in 30 seconds and shares a single link with their client. No signup required for the client.

STEP 02
📤
Upload & version files

Upload deliverables. ClientFlow auto-versions — no more confusing "final_v2_FINAL.pdf" filenames.

STEP 03
💬
Collect feedback in context

Clients leave inline comments on files. All feedback is threaded and timestamped — no email digging needed.

STEP 04
💸
Send & track invoices

One-click invoice creation. Auto-reminders chase late payers. Clients pay directly from the portal.

Core Features
📁
Smart File Versioning

Every upload is auto-versioned. "Latest" is always prominent. Old versions stay accessible but not in the way.

💬
Inline Feedback Threads

Clients annotate directly on PDFs, images, and docs. Feedback is tied to a specific version forever.

📄
One-click Invoicing

Create professional invoices from project scope. Auto-reminders, payment tracking, and PDF export built in.

🔔
Smart Nudges

Auto-nudge clients when feedback is overdue. Freelancers get notified the moment a client acts.
